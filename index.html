Methodology: Each recommendation's UHRI theme tags are mapped to broader rights categories. The radar chart shows how many recommendations fall into each category, allowing comparison across time windows. Developed based on the scripts from Szoszkiewicz & Kaplane (2025).

📋 Rule of Law Taxonomy
This dataset uses a classification taxonomy developed by the Venice Commission (European Commission for Democracy through Law) in the Updated Rule of Law Checklist (CDL-AD(2025)002). The checklist identifies core elements of the rule of law — legality, legal certainty, prevention of abuse of power, equality and non-discrimination, and access to justice — as a framework for assessing compliance with rule of law standards. Here, this taxonomy is applied to classify UN human rights recommendations sourced from the Universal Human Rights Index.

UN Human Rights Analytics Dashboard

Analytics and classification layer built on the OHCHR Universal Human Rights Index
Data source

Built on the dataset published by the Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR) at the Universal Human Rights Index — all country-specific observations and recommendations from Treaty Bodies, Special Procedures, and the Universal Periodic Review. Refreshed monthly. You can also upload your own UHRI export (ohchr.org/data) or any tabular dataset via the 📂 icon.

Cleaned dataset status

The default view shows the cleaned dataset — OCR fixes applied, section headings searchable. The header pill switches between cleaned and the original UHRI export. Full details: pipeline methodology.

Spot something wrong? Use the 🚩 Report issue button on any record detail view — or click below to file a report now. Reports are reviewed during the next monthly refresh and cited in the changelog.

🔒 Offline & Private Mode

Download the full dataset to your browser once — after that every search, filter, and advanced regex query runs entirely on your machine. No queries reach the server. Required for training your own classifier. Recommended on a desktop or laptop; see details in the dialog.
